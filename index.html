06 / Why dehydration?

A process explained
without inflated claims.

Less water.
More utility.

Moisture
removed
  1. 01

    Moisture removal

    Drying reduces water content through controlled heat and airflow.

  2. 02

    Preservation

    Lower available moisture can help limit conditions that support spoilage; outcomes depend on process and packaging.

  3. 03

    Reduced weight

    Removing water reduces product mass, which can change storage and transport needs.

  4. 04

    Food applications

    Dried ingredients can become flakes, powders, mixes, snacks, and easy-to-cook formats.
